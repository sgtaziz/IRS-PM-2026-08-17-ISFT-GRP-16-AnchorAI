Why M0106?

High churn risk (99%), mainly change in weekly visits (this month vs lifetime) and months as a member. High-risk month-to-month members get a discounted month, after the other offers (P05). Of the 10 most similar past members, 3 of 4 who got one month at 20% off stayed, against 4 of 6 with no offer. Estimated effect: +11 points on the chance of staying.
